Current Agricultural Context & Challenges

Bangladesh's legal framework is anchored in the Pesticide Act of 2018, but implementation and enforcement remain weak, creating uncertainty for sustainable crop-protection investment. Researchers identified 25 highly hazardous pesticides still registered for use, ten of them widely used and linked to cancer, kidney failure, and reproductive disorders.

Regulatory Reform & Registration Pathways

Short-term Actions

  • Finalize updated Pesticide Rules under the 2018 Act with FAO support.
  • Establish transparent registration with risk-based review.
  • Align with ASEAN biocontrol guidelines.

Medium-term Reforms

  • Create a stand-alone biopesticide definition or dedicated chapter in law.
  • Introduce provisional, emergency-use, and minor-use registration categories.
  • Enable parallel and equivalent registration for trusted jurisdictions.

Supply Chain & Manufacturing Incentives

Short-term

  • • Train local manufacturers in production and business models.
  • • Partner with distributors and cooperatives for last-mile access.
  • • Engage aggregators to build demand understanding.

Medium-term

  • • Standardize production protocols.
  • • Establish a national microbial biobank.
  • • Build climate-controlled storage and transport.

Farmer Adoption & Extension Services

Farmers doubt biopesticide efficacy because action is slower and performance depends on conditions. Extension services, demonstration farms, and economic incentives are central to behavior change.

Global Lessons: What Works, What Failed

🇹🇭

Thailand — Fast-Track

Cut registration to 6–12 months; exempted low-risk products from toxicology tests.

📌 Replicate the fast-track and publish a minimum-risk exempt list in Year 1.

🇹🇿

Tanzania — Legal Clarity

Created three legal categories (synthetic / bio / biochemical) with stand-alone definitions.

📌 Embed the three-category definition into the final Pesticide Rules within Year 1.

🇮🇳

India — Cautionary Tale

970+ registered companies yet only ~5% of pesticide use. Solved supply, failed demand.

⚠ Commit 30%+ of budget to farmer-facing interventions from Day 1.

🇪🇺

EU — Political Backlash

Achieved 58% pesticide reduction, but the SUR law was abandoned in 2024 amid farmer protests.

⚠ Pair any reduction target with guaranteed supply and farmer compensation.
