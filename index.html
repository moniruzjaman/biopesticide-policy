SubjectFast-track biopesticide registration to protect export earnings, forex & farmer health

The Ask

Approve a provisional biopesticide registration pathway and a 12-month adoption pilot within 90 days.

Why Now — The Cost of Inaction

  • Export consignments are rejected for pesticide residue, costing forex and premium-market access; post-harvest losses total an estimated US$2.4 billion/yr.
  • 68% of farmers use officially banned pesticides and 93% are unaware of the bans — a compounding health and trade liability.
  • Insecticide application has surged +80% in five years while harvests remain uncertain; Thailand and Vietnam are already capturing the regional lead.

Alignment & Support

Advances existing commitments — Smart Bangladesh / Vision 2041, Delta Plan 2100, export diversification & MRL compliance, import substitution, and youth employment. Backed by exporters (FBCCI), FAO/GEF, and public-health advocates; structured as an added fast-track — not an overnight chemical ban — to avoid the backlash that halted the EU's pesticide regulation.

First 100 Days (Visible Wins)

  • Day 60 — Task force constituted with named chair.
  • Day 90 — 10 priority biopesticides registered via provisional pathway.
  • Day 100 — Launch 3 district demonstration farms with residue/yield results.
